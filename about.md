# About This Project

Hello and welcome! 👋  
I'm **Chouaib**, a passionate digital problem-solver from Morocco, focused on improving the visibility, usability, and social impact of online projects — especially those built by indie creators.

This site was launched to share practical strategies in:
- ⚙️ Technical SEO for lightweight sites  
- 🔐 Minimal security improvements  
- 🌍 International job hunting and migration tips  

## 🔍 Why This Project Exists

Many digital creators rely on simplified tools like GitHub Pages, Neocities, or Carrd — yet struggle with poor search visibility, basic security risks, and unclear paths toward global work opportunities.

I aim to change that by:
- Explaining complex topics with clarity  
- Offering lightweight fixes that create real impact  
- Empowering others through free and accessible knowledge  

## 🛠️ Behind the Scenes

No complex frameworks. No commercial fluff.  
Just clean HTML, Markdown, and structured metadata — built to respect your time and improve your reach.

You're welcome to explore, share, and connect. And if you’d like a custom audit or article, head over to [Projects & Services](projects.md).  
Thanks for visiting!
