# SEO Clinic: Hidden Errors on Indie Sites ⚠️

Most indie websites — especially those built with GitHub Pages, Neocities, or Carrd — are fast and lightweight, but often suffer from invisible SEO errors that block indexing and tank visibility.

This guide identifies the 5 most common issues and shows how to fix them.

---

## 1. Missing or Duplicate `<title>` Tags

Your title is the first thing search engines read. If it's missing or duplicated across pages, it confuses crawlers and weakens your ranking.

✅ **Fix:**  
Make sure each page has a unique `<title>` inside the HTML or Markdown front matter.

```html
<title>Secure Portfolio - Indie Dev</title>
